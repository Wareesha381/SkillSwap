Learning Is Better When We Share It

SkillSwap began with a simple question: what if people could use the skills they already have to learn something new?

Many people have knowledge and abilities that could help others, while wanting to learn what someone else already knows. SkillSwap connects these two needs through mutual learning.

Our platform allows people to discover skills, explore possible exchanges and learn from each other.

SkillSwap is a front-end educational prototype that demonstrates the idea of collaborative learning.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

We want to encourage people to share their knowledge and learn new skills through mutual learning.

Our Vision

We imagine a community where everyone has something to teach and everyone has something new to learn.

Our Core Values

At SkillSwap, we believe in collaboration, respect, equality, and creativity. We encourage everyone to share their knowledge, learn new skills, and support each other. Our goal is to create a friendly and supportive community where everyone gets an equal opportunity to grow and succeed.

Community Guidelines

Let's create a respectful, safe and supportive learning community.

  1. Be Respectful

    Respect other people's time, opinions and learning preferences.

  2. Respect Privacy

    Never share passwords or private information.

  3. Give Constructive Feedback

    Help others improve instead of criticising them.
