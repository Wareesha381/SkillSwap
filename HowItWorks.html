How SkillSwap Works

Trade what you know for what you want to learn. Share your skills, meet new people, and grow together without exchanging money.

Get Started Free

Five Simple Steps

Getting started with SkillSwap is easy. Follow these five steps to begin your learning journey.

  1. Create Your Account

    Sign up with your email address and complete the account verification process. Follow SkillSwap's age requirements and account rules.

  2. Build Your Profile

    Add a short introduction to your profile. List the skills you can teach and want to learn. Share only the personal information needed to connect with others.

  3. Find Your Match

    Browse available skills and discover members with similar interests. Find people who can teach what you want to learn and learn what you can teach.

  4. Agree on a Skill Swap

    Contact your match and discuss what you will teach, session duration, and whether you will meet online or in person. Agree on the details before starting.

  5. Learn, Teach, and Review

    Share your knowledge, learn a new skill, and exchange feedback. Leave an honest and respectful review.

Why Choose SkillSwap?

  • Free Learning: Exchange skills instead of paying for lessons.
  • Learn Together: Connect with people who have different skills and experiences.
  • Flexible Sessions: Arrange learning sessions online or in person.
  • Grow Your Network: Share knowledge and build new connections.

Stay Safe While Swapping

A positive learning experience starts with respect and safety. Follow these simple guidelines:

  • Be respectful and honest with other members.
  • Never share passwords, banking details, or verification codes.
  • Avoid sharing your home address or sensitive personal information.
  • Meet in public places and inform someone you trust.
  • Report suspicious behaviour or violations of community rules.

Read our Community Guidelines for more information about safe and respectful skill exchanges.

Your Privacy and Data

Protect your personal information and learn how your data is handled. Read our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for more details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SkillSwap free to use?

SkillSwap lets members exchange skills without paying each other for lessons. Any optional paid features should be clearly identified before use.

Do I need to be an expert?

No. You can teach a skill you understand well enough to help someone else. Be honest about your experience.

Can I learn skills online?

Yes. You can arrange online sessions with other members, subject to the platform's rules.

What if a skill swap does not work out?

You can respectfully end the arrangement. Report any violation of the community rules through the available reporting options.

How can I delete my account?

Check your account settings or contact the platform for assistance. Refer to the Privacy Policy for information about your data.
